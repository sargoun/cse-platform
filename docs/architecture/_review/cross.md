# Cross-document contradictions


======================================================================
## AREA: rechte
======================================================================

### VERDICT
The four K-18 scopes are the one part of my area that is genuinely clean: all thirteen documents carry the same `mandant | gruppe | person | kunde` vocabulary, the same `sitzung_ansicht` enum, the same `app.mandant_id`-is-NULL-in-three-scopes rule, and — checked route by route — no employee- or customer-facing route anywhere runs under group scope. `/portal/mein` is `withPersonScope` and `/portal/kunde` is `withKundeScope` in every document that names them, and every `/portal/gruppe` route in 04-SEITENKARTE is gated on a `gruppe.*` key. Invariant 10 (no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE policy naming a multi-tenant scope) is stated identically everywhere.

The permission catalogue is the opposite: it does not hold together. Eighteen real contradictions, and roughly forty right keys used in RLS policies or route gates that have no row in 03-AUTH-BERECHTIGUNGEN.md's catalogue. Because `app.hat_recht()` returns **false** for an unknown key by design, every one of those is a silent, permanent zero-row failure rather than an error — exactly the failure mode CLAUDE.md's invariants exist to prevent.

Four are structural and block Phase 1:
1. **01-KERN.md §6.6 denies that module `dienstplan` exists** and assigns `dienstplan.arbzg_pruefen` to a module `einsatz` that appears in no other document. 03-AUTH's 46-module vocabulary is the one every other document writes against.
2. **01-KERN.md §4's `berechtigung_aktion` has eight values and omits `schreiben` and `pruefen`.** K-03's `WITH CHECK` names `<modul>.schreiben` on every tenant table in every domain, and 01-KERN's own Phase-1 seed inserts a key whose action is `pruefen`. As written, no write path in the platform can be authorised.
3. **The K-18 customer policy is keyed on the wrong accessor in four documents.** 03-GEWERKE, 04-PLANUNG-ZEIT, 06-RADAR and 05-API-KARTE write `kunde_id = app.aktueller_kunde()`, which resolves through `app.aktiver_mandant()` — NULL in kunde scope. The customer portal reads zero rows.
4. **`app.portal` has no defined value in three of the four scopes.** 01-KERN's `app.portal_fuer(benutzer, mandant)` is called with the session's `aktiver_mandant_id`, which the `benutzer_sitzung` CHECK forces to NULL in gruppe, person and kunde scope, so it falls through to the fail-closed `'mitarbeiter'` — firing every `p_ma_ceiling` in the group view and ceilinging every customer as an employee. Only 05-API-KARTE states the four-branch derivation that works.

Two more are worth flagging as policy-level disagreements rather than typos: 03-AUTH bans `app.portal() = 'intern'` from every policy and CI-tests for it, while four data-model documents enumerate `p_intern_ceiling` on well over a hundred tables; and 03-GEWERKE §1.7 declares a `mitarbeiter` read-right matrix that is "binding on 03-AUTH-BERECHTIGUNGEN.md" and directly contradicts 03-AUTH §12.7's "a worker holds no read right at all".

The cleanest sequencing: fix 01-KERN §4 and §6.6 first (they gate everything else), then settle the four accessor/derivation items, then run one pass over 06-RADAR §1.3 and 03-AUTH §7.4/§12/§14 to reconcile the catalogue — the majority of the dangling keys are concentrated in those two sections.

### VERDICT
The documents do NOT agree on table and column names. I found 30 genuine contradictions and 37 dangling references in my area. Eight table names are referenced but defined in no 02-datenmodell file: `job_lauf`, `mandant_einstellung`, `steuersatz`, `rechnung_beziehung`, `agent_artefakt`, `sicherheitsvorfall`, `nachweis_art` and `loeschprotokoll` — `job_lauf` is the worst, because four documents write it with four different column sets (`job_schluessel`/`job`, `begonnen_am`/`gestartet_am`, `befund`/`kennzahlen`, `status`/`ergebnis`) and two of them disagree on whether it has a `mandant_id` at all, which also collides with the platform-wide claim that `audit_log` is the only tenant-adjacent table with a nullable one.
